HELPFUL INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions.

How do I request a quote?

Complete the quote form with your pickup and delivery locations, service, shipment details, and timing. It opens your email app with a draft for you to review and send. No request is sent until you send that email.

Is shipment tracking live on this website?

No. This website is not connected to carrier systems and does not retrieve shipment locations. Contact Oluku by phone or email for an update.

Are all services available for every route?

Availability depends on the route, shipment, and handling requirements. Oluku confirms service options and pricing for each request before booking.
