Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This notice describes information associated with your use of the Oluku LLC website at olukullc.com and when you contact us through it. It covers this website, not separate services or transactions that may have their own privacy terms.

Information you choose to send

When you submit the quote form, the information you provide—such as your name, email address, phone number, pickup and delivery locations, service selection, approximate weight, and shipment details—is sent to Formspree, a third-party form processor, to transmit your request to Oluku. Oluku uses the request to respond to your inquiry and manage related business communications.

Do not include payment-card details, passwords, government identification numbers, or sensitive documents in a quote request.

Third-party form processor

Formspree processes form submissions and may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, access time, and referring website to operate and protect its service. Formspree's own privacy terms govern its handling of this information. Review the Formspree Privacy Policy for details about its collection, use, and retention practices.

Demonstration tracking form

The tracking form is a demonstration and is not connected to carrier systems. The value you enter is handled in the page in your browser to display the demonstration response; the website does not send it to Oluku or use it to look up a shipment. Avoid entering confidential shipment information.

Technical information and hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When your browser requests a page or file, GitHub and network providers may process technical information such as your IP address, requested URL, request time, browser or device information, and referring page in connection with delivering, protecting, and maintaining the service. Their handling and retention are governed by their own privacy terms and practices. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The site itself does not set cookies for its quote or tracking features and does not currently include analytics tools or advertising pixels. Your browser may cache site files for normal performance. If analytics, advertising, cookies, or other data-collection features are added, this notice will be updated to describe them.

Sharing and retention

Quote requests are shared with Formspree to deliver them to Oluku. Formspree and the website host may process information as described above. Oluku retains business correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage business records, and meet legal obligations, then deletes or securely disposes of it when appropriate.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to submit a quote request and can contact Oluku by phone instead. You may contact us to ask about or request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted to Oluku; we will respond as applicable law requires and subject to legitimate recordkeeping obligations. For information held by Formspree, please also consult its privacy policy. Privacy rights vary by location.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Oluku LLC at lukeman.ibrahim@live.com or (240) 978-3987.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the website or its data practices change. The current version and its update date will be posted on this page.
